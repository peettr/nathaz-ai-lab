1. 模型与公式（逐条给出处）

准定常横向力。物体以速度 ẏ 横向运动时，它「看到」的相对来流偏转一个角度，tan α = ẏ/U。假设此刻的气动力等于静止物体在该攻角下测到的力：

Fy = ½ ρ U² B · CFy(α)，  CFy = −[CL(α) + CD(α) tan α] / cos α

Simiu & Yeo, Wind Effects on Structures, 第 4 版, 2019, 式 (20.4)–(20.7)，书页 297（PDF 第 323 页）。该书取 y 向下为正；Holmes 的写法取 z 向上为正、攻角变化 Δα = −ż/U（式 5.45 上方）。两种符号约定描述的是同一件事，本页图中 y 向上为正，标的攻角是 α = −ẏ/U。

运动方程与净阻尼。

m [ ÿ + 2ζω1ẏ + ω1² y ] = Fy ≈ −½ ρ U² B · G · ẏ/U，  G = (dCL/dα + CD)α=0
d = 2mω1ζ + ½ ρ U B · G

Simiu & Yeo 2019, 式 (20.8)、(20.11)–(20.13)，书页 298（PDF 第 324 页）。

Glauert–Den Hartog 判据。d < 0 时失稳（充分条件）；因为 ζ > 0，必须先有 G < 0（必要条件）。圆柱 dCL/dα = 0，所以不会驰振。

Simiu & Yeo 2019, 式 (20.14) 及其后一段，书页 299（PDF 第 325 页）；Holmes & Bekele, Wind Loading of Structures, 第 4 版, 式 (5.45) 后「den Hartog's criterion」，书页 182（PDF 第 203 页）；Parkinson & Smith 1964, 式 (18) 后，第 231 页（PDF 第 7 页）。

起振风速与 Scruton 数。令 d = 0：

Ucr = −8π ζ n1 m / (ρ B G)，  Ucr/(n1B) = 2 Sc / (−G)，  Sc = 4π ζ m / (ρ B²)

Holmes & Bekele 第 4 版, 式 (5.46)、(5.47)、(5.48)，书页 182–183（PDF 第 203–204 页）。原书式 (5.48) 排印缺 π 和 η（印成「4 struct m」），这里按式 (5.46)→(5.47) 反推补回。同一结果的另外两种写法：Parkinson & Smith 式 (19) U0 = 2β/(nA)，第 231 页；Naudascher & Rockwell 式 (7.22)、(7.24) (Vr)cr = 4π Sc/[dCy/dα]，其中 Sc = 2mζ/(ρd²)，书页 186–187（PDF 第 205–206 页）。各书 Scruton 数的定义差一个常数因子，本页用 Holmes 的定义。

非线性横向力多项式。要得到振幅，线性化不够，需要 CFy 随 ẏ/U 的整条曲线：

CFy = A1(ẏ/U) − A3(ẏ/U)³ + A5(ẏ/U)⁵ − A7(ẏ/U)⁷，  A1 = dCFy/dα|0 = −G

Parkinson & Smith 1964, 式 (2)，第 227 页（PDF 第 3 页），原文记作 A、B、C、D；方柱系数 A = 2.69、B = 168、C = 6270、D = 59900 见图 2 图题，第 228 页（PDF 第 4 页），光滑流、Re = 22 300、拟合范围 −16° < α < 16°。Simiu & Yeo 式 (20.15)，书页 300（PDF 第 326 页）是同一形式（另含偶次项 A2），引自 Novak 1972 式 (2)，第 28 页（PDF 第 2 页）。

平均法（Krylov–Bogoliubov 一次近似）。设 y = a cos(ω1t + φ)，a 缓变，对一个周期取平均：

da/dt = a { −ζω1 + (ρ U B / 4m) [ A1 − ¾ A3 q² + ⅝ A5 q⁴ − (35/64) A7 q⁶ ] }，  q = aω1/U
稳态：A1(1 − Ucr/U) − ¾ A3 q² + ⅝ A5 q⁴ − (35/64) A7 q⁶ = 0

Parkinson & Smith 1964, 式 (9)、(10)，第 229 页（PDF 第 5 页）；稳态式 (20)，第 233 页（PDF 第 9 页）；稳定性由 F′(R) 的符号判断，式 (16)、(17)，第 230 页（PDF 第 6 页）。系数 1、¾、⅝、35/64 与 Novak 1972 表 1 的 Br（1.0、0.75、0.625、0.54688）一致，第 31 页（PDF 第 5 页）。本页的式子是把原文的无量纲式换回有量纲量（n = ρh²/2m，β = ζ，U原文 = U/ω1h），换算过程见 README。

归一化后的通用曲线。以 a ω1/Ucr 对 U/Ucr 作图，曲线与质量、阻尼无关（原文图 5，第 236 页，PDF 第 12 页；Novak 1972 结论 6，第 37 页）。高风速时趋于过原点的渐近线，原文给出 Ȳs ≈ 0.3 U（式 25，第 237 页），本页由系数算得 0.293。

3. 准定常假设什么时候成立

  • 准定常意味着气动力与运动同相、没有滞后，这要求物体运动速度远小于来流速度，也就是折减风速 U/(n1B) 足够大。Naudascher & Rockwell 给的量级是「大约大于 20」（§7.2.4，书页 185，PDF 第 204 页）。本页在 Ucr/(n1B) < 20 时给出提示，20 取自这句话，原文是量级性的说法，不是严格界限。
  • 折减风速应明显高于涡脱共振对应的值 1/St。方柱 St = 0.135，对应 U/(n1B) ≈ 7.4（Parkinson & Smith 第 228 页）。阻尼低、起振风速靠近涡振区时，试验点偏离理论曲线，滞回变小（同文第 237 页）。
  • 导数 dCFy/dα 与运动频率无关，可以由静止模型测力得到，这是驰振与颤振的区别（Simiu & Yeo 书页 300，PDF 第 326 页）。

4. 本页的局限

  • 只有横风向一个自由度，二维、均匀光滑来流。湍流会明显改变 CFy 曲线：矩形 2:1 在湍流度 8.5% 以上可以不再驰振，矩形 1:2 反而变得不稳定（Novak 1972 第 39–43 页）。
  • 没有涡激力，也没有涡振与驰振的耦合；低 Scruton 数时两者会相互作用。
  • 方柱多项式只在 |α| < 16° 内拟合；高风速渐近线对应 α ≈ 16.3°，已在拟合范围边缘，页面在超出时会提示。
  • 只有方柱有完整的文献多项式。其余预设只做线性判据，时程是线性模型（没有极限环，振幅到显示上限即停）。硬激励型驰振（矩形 1:2、D 形）未建模。
  • 为了让动画在越过 Ucr 后能自己起振，积分里保留了 0.002B 的背景扰动；截面动画的摆动节奏是放慢显示的，振幅和攻角是实时积分值。
  • 系数随雷诺数、棱角圆角、展弦比、端部条件变化；页面数值只对应原文的试验条件，不能直接用于设计。

1. Model and formulas (with sources)

Quasi-steady lateral force. A body moving across the flow with velocity ẏ sees the relative wind turned through an angle with tan α = ẏ/U. The force at that instant is assumed equal to the force measured on the fixed body at that angle of attack:

Fy = ½ ρ U² B · CFy(α),  CFy = −[CL(α) + CD(α) tan α] / cos α

Simiu & Yeo, Wind Effects on Structures, 4th ed., 2019, Eqs. (20.4)–(20.7), p. 297 (PDF p. 323). That book takes y positive downward; Holmes takes z positive upward with Δα = −ż/U (above Eq. 5.45). Both conventions describe the same physics. In the sketch on this page y is positive upward and the angle shown is α = −ẏ/U.

Equation of motion and net damping.

m [ ÿ + 2ζω1ẏ + ω1² y ] = Fy ≈ −½ ρ U² B · G · ẏ/U,  G = (dCL/dα + CD)α=0
d = 2mω1ζ + ½ ρ U B · G

Simiu & Yeo 2019, Eqs. (20.8), (20.11)–(20.13), p. 298 (PDF p. 324).

Glauert–Den Hartog criterion. The system is unstable when d < 0 (sufficient condition); since ζ > 0 this requires G < 0 (necessary condition). For a circular cylinder dCL/dα = 0, so it cannot gallop.

Simiu & Yeo 2019, Eq. (20.14) and the following paragraph, p. 299 (PDF p. 325); Holmes & Bekele, Wind Loading of Structures, 4th ed., after Eq. (5.45), p. 182 (PDF p. 203); Parkinson & Smith 1964, after Eq. (18), p. 231 (PDF p. 7).

Onset speed and Scruton number. Setting d = 0:

Ucr = −8π ζ n1 m / (ρ B G),  Ucr/(n1B) = 2 Sc / (−G),  Sc = 4π ζ m / (ρ B²)

Holmes & Bekele 4th ed., Eqs. (5.46), (5.47), (5.48), pp. 182–183 (PDF pp. 203–204). Eq. (5.48) is misprinted in the book (π and η are missing); it is restored here from Eqs. (5.46)→(5.47). Equivalent forms: Parkinson & Smith Eq. (19), U0 = 2β/(nA), p. 231; Naudascher & Rockwell Eqs. (7.22), (7.24), (Vr)cr = 4π Sc/[dCy/dα] with Sc = 2mζ/(ρd²), pp. 186–187 (PDF pp. 205–206). Definitions of the Scruton number differ by constant factors between books; this page uses Holmes's.

Nonlinear lateral-force polynomial. The amplitude cannot be obtained from the linearised model; the whole CFy curve is needed:

CFy = A1(ẏ/U) − A3(ẏ/U)³ + A5(ẏ/U)⁵ − A7(ẏ/U)⁷,  A1 = dCFy/dα|0 = −G

Parkinson & Smith 1964, Eq. (2), p. 227 (PDF p. 3), written there with A, B, C, D; square-prism values A = 2.69, B = 168, C = 6270, D = 59900 are in the caption of Fig. 2, p. 228 (PDF p. 4): smooth flow, Re = 22,300, fitted for −16° < α < 16°. Simiu & Yeo Eq. (20.15), p. 300 (PDF p. 326) has the same form (with an extra even term A2), taken from Novak 1972 Eq. (2), p. 28 (PDF p. 2).

Averaging (first approximation of Krylov–Bogoliubov). With y = a cos(ω1t + φ), a slowly varying, averaged over one cycle:

da/dt = a { −ζω1 + (ρ U B / 4m) [ A1 − ¾ A3 q² + ⅝ A5 q⁴ − (35/64) A7 q⁶ ] },  q = aω1/U
steady state: A1(1 − Ucr/U) − ¾ A3 q² + ⅝ A5 q⁴ − (35/64) A7 q⁶ = 0

Parkinson & Smith 1964, Eqs. (9), (10), p. 229 (PDF p. 5); steady state Eq. (20), p. 233 (PDF p. 9); stability from the sign of F′(R), Eqs. (16), (17), p. 230 (PDF p. 6). The factors 1, ¾, ⅝, 35/64 agree with Br in Table 1 of Novak 1972 (1.0, 0.75, 0.625, 0.54688), p. 31 (PDF p. 5). The expressions here are the paper's dimensionless equations converted back to dimensional quantities (n = ρh²/2m, β = ζ, Upaper = U/ω1h); the conversion is written out in the README.

Universal curve. Plotting a ω1/Ucr against U/Ucr gives one curve for all masses and damping levels (Fig. 5 of the paper, p. 236, PDF p. 12; Novak 1972, conclusion 6, p. 37). At high wind speed it approaches an asymptote through the origin; the paper gives Ȳs ≈ 0.3 U (Eq. 25, p. 237), and the coefficients give 0.293.

3. When the quasi-steady assumption holds

  • Quasi-steady means the force is in phase with the motion, with no lag. This needs the body velocity to be small compared with the flow velocity, i.e. a large reduced velocity U/(n1B). Naudascher & Rockwell give the order of magnitude “say greater than about 20” (§7.2.4, p. 185, PDF p. 204). This page shows a caution when Ucr/(n1B) < 20; the number comes from that sentence and is an order of magnitude, not a sharp limit.
  • The reduced velocity should be well above the vortex-resonance value 1/St. For a square, St = 0.135, so U/(n1B) ≈ 7.4 (Parkinson & Smith p. 228). At low damping, where onset is close to vortex resonance, the test points depart from the theory and the hysteresis shrinks (same paper, p. 237).
  • The derivative dCFy/dα does not depend on the frequency of motion and can be measured on a fixed model; this is what distinguishes galloping from flutter (Simiu & Yeo p. 300, PDF p. 326).

4. Limitations of this page

  • One transverse degree of freedom, two-dimensional, uniform smooth flow. Turbulence changes the CFy curve markedly: the 2:1 rectangle can stop galloping above about 8.5% turbulence intensity, while the 1:2 rectangle becomes unstable (Novak 1972, pp. 39–43).
  • No vortex-induced force and no interaction between vortex shedding and galloping; they interact at low Scruton number.
  • The square-prism polynomial is fitted only for |α| < 16°; the high-speed asymptote corresponds to α ≈ 16.3°, at the edge of the fit. The page flags this when it happens.
  • Only the square has a complete published polynomial. Other presets use the linear criterion only, and their time history is the linear model (no limit cycle; the run stops at the display limit). Hard-excitation galloping (1:2 rectangle, D-section) is not modelled.
  • A background disturbance of 0.002B is kept in the integration so that the motion can start by itself once U exceeds Ucr. The sketch oscillates at a slowed-down display rate; its amplitude and angle are the live integrated values.
  • Coefficients depend on Reynolds number, corner radius, aspect ratio and end conditions. The numbers here belong to the test conditions of the source papers and must not be used directly for design.
