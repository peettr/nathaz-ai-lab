Across-wind galloping of a square prism: the Glauert–Den Hartog criterion and critical wind speed (teaching demo v0.1)

An interactive teaching demo: single degree of freedom, quasi-steady, linearized (onset or decay only). Move the sliders to see when a square prism gallops; formula sources are at the bottom of the page. · 中文

The default G = −2 is an assumed value for the demo, not a measurement from any particular test; for a real square prism G depends on section details and turbulence intensity and must be obtained from wind-tunnel CL(α) and CD(α).

How this page computes (all simplifications are listed here)
Equation of motion (Simiu & Yeo 2019, Eq. 20.12): m[ÿ + 2ζω1ẏ + ω1²y] = −½ρU²B·G·(ẏ/U), ω1 = 2πn1.
Net damping coefficient (Eq. 20.13): d = 2mω1ζ + ½ρUB·G. d < 0 means instability (sufficient condition); G < 0 is the necessary condition (Eq. 20.14, the Glauert–Den Hartog criterion). For a circular cylinder dCL/dα = 0 and CD > 0, so G > 0 and it cannot gallop (same book, same page). The page uses G = CD = 1.2 for the cylinder as an illustrative value; the conclusion holds for any CD > 0.
Critical wind speed (setting d = 0): Ucr = −4mω1ζ/(ρB·G) = −8πζn1m/(ρB·G), consistent with Holmes & Bekele, Wind Loading of Structures, 4th ed., Eq. (5.46); in terms of the Scruton number: Ucr/(n1B) = 2Sc/(−G), Sc = 4πζm/(ρB²) (Eqs. 5.47, 5.48).
Not included: (1) limit-cycle amplitude, which needs higher-order polynomial coefficients of CFy(α) (Simiu & Yeo Eq. 20.15, Parkinson–Smith type) that the sources used here do not give, so only linearized onset/decay is drawn and the curve below is clipped at the frame — it is not a real amplitude; (2) vortex-induced resonance and its coupling with galloping (important at low Scruton numbers); (3) oncoming turbulence, three-dimensional and end effects; (4) the quasi-steady assumption needs a large enough reduced velocity: if Ucr/(n1B) = 2Sc/(−G) is low, the predicted Ucr may be unreliable (it may fall in the vortex-shedding range) and is only qualitative; the page uses 20 as a warning line, an illustrative value with no literature basis.
Sources: Simiu & Yeo, Wind Effects on Structures, 4th ed., 2019, §20.1.1, Eqs. 20.8–20.14; Holmes & Bekele, Wind Loading of Structures, 4th ed., 2021, §5.5.2, Eqs. 5.44–5.48.
